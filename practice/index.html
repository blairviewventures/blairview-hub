Consumer product judgment.

Seventeen years of it, across four companies that had to earn their way in with real users. Consumer products don't get a grace period. People either come back or they don't. That constraint shapes how I think about the work: what to ship now versus what to validate, where to give users choices and where that's just friction, what's worth defending in a roadmap review and what isn't.

Four companies, four very different categories, one underlying discipline. The judgment calls that made HBO GO work before streaming had a playbook are the same ones that close a retention gap at a Series B. That transfer, from one consumer context to another, is what I spend most engagements doing.

2011 – 2013

Streaming · Media

HBO GO across 11 platforms

Launched HBO GO across 11 platforms before streaming was a recognized category. 0→1, no playbook. Web, iPad, Xbox, Samsung Smart TVs, Roku, Kindle Fire, Android, iPhone, PS3, HTML5, Apple TV.

Interactive Emmy nomination, Game of Thrones Enhanced Digital Experience. Television Academy, 2013.

2013 – 2018

Loyalty · Consumer Commerce

Pay with Membership Rewards inside Uber, American Express

The first credit card rewards product to live inside a third-party consumer app. Embedded loyalty mechanics into a product used by hundreds of millions, before embedded finance was a recognized category.

2018 – 2024

AI · Wealth · Product Ops

First production GenAI advisor platform and chatbot experience, Morgan Stanley

Executive Director, Digital Product Management

Crafted the GenAI advisor platform at a top-tier financial institution. Live system serving 16,000+. Proprietary OpenAI partnership.

Owned the advisor desktop and mobile platform end to end, with a full redesign from ideation through launch. Led the division's digital transformation from waterfall to agile across product strategy and operations.

2024 – 2026

Digital Wallets · Consumer Banking

Digital wallets and payments, Chase

Executive Director, Digital Product Management · UX, Proprietary Wallets

Managed the front end digital wallets and payment experience at JPMorgan Chase. Drove product improvements to keep Chase top of mind and top of wallet, everywhere cardholders spent and wanted to spend.

Four companies. One judgment call, repeated. Ship what matters. Cut what doesn’t.
