About

Consumer product judgment.

17 years of it, across four companies that had to earn their way in with real users. Consumer products don't get a grace period. People either come back or they don't. That constraint shapes how I think about the work: what to ship now versus what to validate, where to give users choices and where that's just friction, what's worth defending in a roadmap review and what isn't.

Four companies, four very different categories, one underlying discipline. The judgment calls that made HBO GO work before streaming had a playbook are the same ones that close a retention gap at a Series B. That transfer, from one consumer context to another, is what I spend most engagements doing.

2011 – 2013  ·  Entertainment

HBO GO across 11 platforms

11-platform rollout in rapid succession before streaming had a category. Web, iPad, Xbox, Samsung Smart TVs, Roku, Kindle Fire, Android, iPhone, PS3, HTML5, Apple TV. Interactive Emmy nomination, Television Academy, 2013.

2013 – 2018  ·  Commerce & Loyalty

Pay with Membership Rewards inside the Uber app, American Express

Built the first credit card rewards integration inside a third-party consumer app. Pay with Membership Rewards inside Uber, used by hundreds of millions of cardmembers, before embedded finance was a recognized category.

2018 – 2024  ·  AI & Wealth

First production GenAI advisor platform, Morgan Stanley

First production GenAI platform at a top-tier financial institution. Live system serving 16,000 advisors. Proprietary OpenAI relationship. Full mobile redesign from ideation through launch.

2024 – 2026  ·  Consumer Digital

Consumer payments and digital wallet, Chase

Executive Director, Digital Product Management. Managed the front end digital wallets and payment experience at Chase.
