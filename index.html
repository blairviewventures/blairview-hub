Senior Product Advisory
You have a head of product. The team is executing. But the hardest decisions benefit from a thought partner with pattern recognition across four consumer categories at scale. What gets prioritized. What gets cut. Whether the right people are working on the right problems. I sit in the reviews where those decisions get made, pressure-test the thinking, and push back when no one else will. The result: sharper tradeoff decisions, a prioritization process that holds, and a strategy the leadership team can defend.

Fractional Product Leadership
Your product leader seat is empty, or the work in front of you is bigger than the leadership in place can take on alone. I come in as a leader inside the team you already have, with judgment built from running consumer product at scale across four different companies. What that looks like: running the work day to day, deciding what gets built and what gets cut, breaking it down so people know what they own, and making the hard calls. The result: a launch that lands when leadership says it will, a team that knows where the line is between debate and decision, and work that stands up after I leave.
