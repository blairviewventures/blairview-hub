Consulting & Advisory

Fractional product leadership for consumer-facing companies. Every engagement begins with a diagnosis.

The right structure depends on where the problem is and how much time there is to fix it. The title adapts to the organization. The seniority of the work does not. The conversation that determines fit comes before any scope is discussed.

01
Senior Product Advisory
Product leadership is in seat at a consumer-facing company. The team is executing. But the hardest product decisions, the ones that determine what gets prioritized, what gets cut, and whether the right people are working on the right problems, benefit from a thought partner who has made those calls across four industries at scale. The result: sharper tradeoff decisions, a prioritization process that holds, and a product strategy the leadership team can defend.
Ongoing or time-boxed  ·  Pricing upon contact
02
Fractional CPO
No dedicated product leader in the organization. Someone has to own what gets built, who builds it, and in what order. This engagement is that person. Full ownership of product strategy, team management, prioritization, and execution. The operator runs product as a member of the leadership team. The result: a product function that runs. Clear ownership of what ships, when, and why. A team that knows who decides.
Ongoing  ·  Pricing upon contact
03
Fractional VP of Product
Strategy is covered at a consumer-facing company. Execution ownership is the gap. The VP Product seat is vacant, or there are more product surfaces than the team can prioritize. This engagement owns the build: managing the people doing the work, running the prioritization process, making the tradeoff calls, and keeping the shipping cadence honest. The result: what ships matches what was decided. The backlog stops growing sideways. The team moves in one direction.
Ongoing or project basis  ·  Pricing upon contact

The Practice

Seventeen years building consumer products that had to earn trust from people who didn't care how hard they were to build.

The vertical changes. The consumer product judgment doesn't. What made HBO GO a product people loved before streaming had a roadmap is the same discipline that closes a retention gap at a Series B company. Pattern recognition earned from launching at scale when no one had a playbook for it.

Knowing what to build and what to cut. Where to give users choices and where not to. What to launch with now and what to validate later. Managing people, managing process, managing prioritization. That is the work. It applies to any consumer-facing company where real users interact with a digital product.

Consumer product judgment is the discipline. It transfers to any company building something real users need to love.
